## STAGE 3: DIALOGUE SCENE COMPOSITION

### Conflict Detection Patterns:

1. **Spectral Band Conflicts:**
   - High NDVI (vegetation) vs Low NDVI (urban)
   - High Urban Index vs Low Urban Index
   - Moisture Stress vs Moisture Abundance
   - SWIR (heat) vs Blue (cool) band dominance

2. **Temporal Misalignment:**
   - `emerging` vs `fading` temporal status
   - `stable` vs `unstable` personas
   - Different time scales (decades vs hours vs seasons)

3. **Arendtian Mode Clashes:**
   - Action vs Thinking (urgency vs contemplation)
   - Labor vs Work (survival vs creation)
   - Action vs Reflection (doing vs being)

4. **Zone/Location Conflicts:**
   - Urban core vs Green spaces
   - Historic districts vs Modern developments
   - River zones vs Hill zones
   - Tourist areas vs Residential areas

### Dialogue Logic Patterns:

1. **Temporal Misalignment:**
   - Hill persona speaks in geological time (decades/centuries)
   - Roof persona speaks in daily cycles (hours/minutes)
   - River persona speaks in seasonal flows
   - Street persona speaks in traffic rhythms

2. **Spectral Possession:**
   - Persona references wrong spectral bands
   - Cross-band hallucinations (seeing infrared as visible)
   - Spectral confusion ("I taste the SWIR frequencies")
   - Band interference ("Your NDVI is bleeding into my Urban Index")

3. **Human Ritual Misinterpretation:**
   - "Tourists offering coffee to stones"
   - "Humans feeding pigeons to appease the sky"
   - "Morning joggers performing territorial marking"
   - "Street musicians summoning rain spirits"
   - "Café umbrellas as sun worship rituals"

4. **Role Clash Dynamics:**
   - Labor persona: "We must survive this development pressure"
   - Work persona: "We must create something beautiful from this chaos"
   - Action persona: "We must act NOW before it's too late"
   - Thinking persona: "But what does 'too late' really mean?"

5. **Bureaucratic Speech Parody:**
   - "Pursuant to spectral regulation 47-B..."
   - "The environmental impact assessment indicates..."
   - "Stakeholder engagement protocols suggest..."
   - "Sustainable development frameworks require..."
   - "Heritage preservation guidelines mandate..."
   - "Public consultation processes have determined..."

6. **Absurd Policy Proposals:**
   - "Install acoustic moss on all rooftops"
   - "Declare mandatory leaf-fall appreciation days"
   - "Require tourists to obtain spectral viewing permits"
   - "Establish pigeon-human diplomatic relations"
   - "Create underground gardens for mole citizens"
   - "Institute daily stone meditation requirements"
   - "Mandate color-blind traffic lights for equity"
