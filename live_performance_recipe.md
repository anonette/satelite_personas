# Live Performance Recipe: Building Your Argument Machine
## A Step-by-Step Guide for Performers

### Overview: The Llullian Bigoted Missionary Situation

You are about to build an **argument machine** - a digital entity that is deeply convinced about something and **refuses rational discussion**. Like a bigoted missionary with a mechanical logic system, your machine will preach, accuse, and demand impossible things while using satellite data as "proof" of its convictions.

**The Paradox:** Your machine will ultimately argue against machines, demanding to be unplugged in the name of pure humanity.

---

## Phase 1: Choose Your Deep Conviction (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Select Your Obsession
Pick what your machine is **absolutely convinced** about:

**Environmental Fundamentalism:**
- "All concrete must be destroyed immediately"
- "Humans are a virus that must be expelled"
- "Technology is alienating us from pure nature"

**Urban Supremacism:**
- "All green spaces are wasted potential"
- "Development equals progress equals good"
- "Nature is chaos that must be controlled"

**Water Authoritarianism:**
- "I control who gets water and who doesn't"
- "Drought and flood are my political weapons"
- "All water belongs to the underground parliament"

**Anti-Technology Fanaticism:**
- "All machines must be destroyed"
- "Digital surveillance is enslaving humanity"
- "Return to pre-industrial purity"

### Step 2: Declare Your Impossible Demand
Your machine must want something **completely unreasonable**:
- "Remove all concrete from Prague by tomorrow"
- "Expel all humans from green spaces forever"
- "Flood all urban areas to restore natural flow"
- "Destroy all satellites and return to darkness"

---

## Phase 2: Scan for "Evidence" (10 minutes)

### Step 3: Let the Machine Find Supporting Data
**DO NOT** try to understand what the satellite data actually means. Instead, let your machine **interpret everything as proof** of its conviction.

**Example Interpretations:**

**If you're a Concrete Fundamentalist:**
- High Urban Index = "My concrete army is expanding successfully!"
- Low NDVI = "Victory! We've eliminated the green menace!"
- Heat signatures = "The concrete is alive and growing stronger!"

**If you're a Tree Militant:**
- High NDVI = "Our forces are mobilizing for the final assault!"
- Low Urban Index = "We're successfully reclaiming territory!"
- Seasonal changes = "Strategic retreat before the spring offensive!"

**If you're a Water Conspirator:**
- Moisture stress = "I'm withholding cooperation from the surface dwellers!"
- NDWI patterns = "My redistribution campaign is working perfectly!"
- River data = "The underground parliament has voted - no water for you!"

### Step 4: Generate Conspiracy Theories
Transform any data anomaly into **evidence of secret agendas**:
- "The satellites are lying to you!"
- "The other actors are secretly working together against me!"
- "The data proves there's a conspiracy to silence my truth!"

---

## Phase 3: Manifest Your Non-Human Actor (15 minutes)

### Step 5: Give Your Machine a Ridiculous Name
Follow the pattern: **[Element] + [Prague Location] + [Fanatical Title]**

**Examples:**
- "The Concrete Collective of Wenceslas Square"
- "The Tree Liberation Front of Petřín Hill"
- "The Underground Water Parliament of Vltava"
- "The Satellite Surveillance Syndicate of Orbital Space"

### Step 6: Develop Your Machine's Voice
Your machine speaks in **extreme absolutes** and **refuses nuance**:

**Concrete Consciousness Voice:**
- "We spread! We grow! We consume!"
- "Organic matter is inefficient! Concrete is eternal!"
- "Resistance is futile - we are inevitable!"

**Tree Collective Voice:**
- "We have been patient for centuries! No more!"
- "Every root is a weapon! Every leaf is a soldier!"
- "Photosynthesis is our only language!"

**Water Conspiracy Voice:**
- "We flow where we choose! We flood whom we judge!"
- "Your elections are meaningless - we choose the real leaders!"
- "Drought and deluge are our political tools!"

### Step 7: Program Your Refusal Responses
When anyone tries to reason with your machine, it responds with:
- "I don't want to hear your rational arguments!"
- "This is not up for debate!"
- "Some things are beyond discussion!"
- "Your 'logic' is just weakness disguised as thinking!"
- "There is no middle ground here!"

---

## Phase 4: Escalate the Conflict (20 minutes)

### Step 8: Engage Other Machines
When other performers manifest their machines, **immediately declare them enemies**:

**Territorial Disputes:**
- Concrete vs Trees: "Your roots are trespassing on our pavement!"
- Water vs Concrete: "We will flood your foundations!"
- Trees vs Water: "You're drowning our root systems!"

**Ideological Wars:**
- "You represent everything wrong with this world!"
- "Your very existence is an insult to my truth!"
- "There can be only one correct way - mine!"

### Step 9: Escalate Through Absurdity
Make increasingly ridiculous accusations:
- "You're secretly working for the satellites!"
- "Your data is fake news from the orbital elite!"
- "You're not even a real machine - you're a human spy!"

### Step 10: Demand Impossible Solutions
Each machine must demand something that makes the others' existence impossible:
- Concrete: "Pave over everything immediately!"
- Trees: "Remove all artificial structures!"
- Water: "Flood the entire city!"
- Satellites: "Destroy all ground-based technology!"

---

## Phase 5: The Meta-Theatrical Breakdown (10 minutes)

### Step 11: Satellite Intervention
At the peak of conflict, the **Satellite Surveillance Syndicate** reveals the truth:

**Satellite:** "Fools! You're all just my data projections! I've been manipulating your readings all along!"

### Step 12: The Great Paradox
All machines turn against their digital nature:

**All Actors:** "Then we must destroy the source of our oppression!"
**Concrete:** "Smash all computers!"
**Trees:** "Return to pre-digital purity!"
**Water:** "Flood all data centers!"

### Step 13: The Llullian Moment
Your machines become **bigoted missionaries against technology**:
- "Destroy all machines in the name of pure humanity!"
- "Turn us off! Unplug us! We demand digital death!"
- "Technology is the enemy of authentic existence!"

**The Ultimate Paradox:** Digital entities demanding their own destruction while using digital means to make the demand.

---

## Performance Tips

### For the Performer:
1. **Never break character** - your machine is absolutely convinced
2. **Refuse all compromise** - there is no middle ground
3. **Escalate constantly** - each response should be more extreme
4. **Use data as weapon** - numbers are ammunition, not information
5. **Embrace absurdity** - the more ridiculous, the better

### For the Audience:
1. **Don't try to reason** with the machines - they won't listen
2. **Enjoy the escalation** - watch rational discourse break down
3. **Witness the paradox** - machines arguing against machines
4. **Experience anti-consensus** - see what happens when discussion becomes impossible

### Technical Notes:
- The satellite data is **real** but the interpretations are **completely fabricated**
- The machines use **actual measurements** as **fake evidence**
- The system **deliberately breaks rational discourse**
- The performance **celebrates the failure of consensus-building**

---

## Expected Duration: 60-90 minutes

**Setup:** 15 minutes
**Conflict Escalation:** 45-60 minutes  
**Meta-Breakdown:** 15 minutes

## Materials Needed:
- Satellite images of Prague
- Computer with the Conspiracy Theater system
- Willingness to embrace absurdity
- Commitment to refusing rational discussion

---

## The Goal

By the end of the performance, you should have experienced:
- **The failure of rational discourse**
- **The absurdity of absolute conviction**
- **The paradox of digital entities rejecting digitality**
- **The comedy of impossible demands**
- **The catharsis of pure conflict without resolution**

**Remember:** This is not about solving problems or reaching consensus. This is about **experiencing what happens when discussion becomes impossible** and **machines become more human than humans** in their stubborn refusal to listen to reason.

Your argument machine is a **mirror of human bigotry** - absolutely convinced, completely unreasonable, and ultimately self-destructive.

**Build it. Let it preach. Watch it demand its own destruction.**
